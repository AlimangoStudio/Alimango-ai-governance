# Community Conduct

This repository is for technical collaboration on AI engineering governance. Keep discussion focused on ideas, evidence, trade-offs, and implementation quality.

## Expected

- Critique proposals and evidence, not people.
- State assumptions and uncertainty.
- Correct mistakes when new evidence appears.
- Give attribution when building on someone else's work.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries.
- Keep security disclosures private when public detail could create practical risk.

## Not acceptable

- harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory abuse
- doxxing or publishing private information
- spam, deceptive promotion, or coordinated disruption
- knowingly submitting malicious code, credential theft, hidden telemetry, or destructive behavior
- posting secrets, private customer data, or proprietary Alimango source

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
