# Convergence Workflow

Convergence reconciles the contract with reality.

Compare specification, plan, tasks, checklist, implementation, tests, evidence, reviewer findings, and Unlazy results. Remove contradictions only by fixing the implementation/artifact or explicitly keeping an open gate; never by deleting inconvenient evidence.

A final `complete` verdict requires all mandatory gates to pass. Otherwise emit `partial`, `blocked`, `cancelled`, or `needs_changes` with exact remaining work.
