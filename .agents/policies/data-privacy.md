# Data and Privacy Policy

Classify data before including it in prompts, tools, caches, logs, telemetry, examples, issues, or PRs.

This public repository accepts public/synthetic data only. Secrets, credentials, private source, customer data, patient/health data, personal records, private tenant information, and proprietary project internals are prohibited.

For general governance design, minimize collection, minimize retention, use references/hashes instead of raw sensitive payloads, and separate public research telemetry from production/private data.
