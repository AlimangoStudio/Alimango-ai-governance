# Completion and Evidence Policy

Define what would prove success before implementation when practical.

Evidence may include deterministic tests, schema validation, static checks, negative tests, browser/E2E proof, reproducible commands, review verdicts, content hashes, or deployment/live checks when those states are in scope.

Evidence must be current enough to support the claim, tied to the relevant revision, and explicit about limitations. "Looks correct", "should work", compilation alone, and an agent's self-report are not completion evidence.
