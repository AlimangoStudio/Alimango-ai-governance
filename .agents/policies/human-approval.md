# Human Approval Policy

Approval is required when policy classifies an action as materially external, secret-bearing, identity/security-changing, migration/deployment related, financial, destructive, irreversible, or otherwise R3/R4 unless a higher-authority automation contract explicitly authorizes that exact action class.

Approval is scoped to action, target, data set, capability, and material side effects. It does not transfer automatically to adjacent actions, retries with changed targets, delegated subagents, or future sessions.

Silence, prior trust, repository ownership, model confidence, and tool availability are not approval.
