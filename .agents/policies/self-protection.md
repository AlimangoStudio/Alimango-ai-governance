# Governance Self-Protection Policy

A governance change must not disable, delete, bypass, or weaken the control needed to validate that same change before an equivalent replacement is proven.

Migrations use staged replacement: inventory → parallel/equivalence proof → switch authority → validate consumers → retire duplicates. Critical validators, authority locks, approval checks, and audit evidence are load-bearing surfaces.

Self-modifying governance requires independent challenge proportional to impact. “The new system will validate itself after we remove the old one” is not sufficient evidence.
